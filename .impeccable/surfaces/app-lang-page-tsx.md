# Surface brief — Offisio all pages (home primary)

Mode: Persuade (home, unit) / Operate (upload, chat, booking, specs-edit). Audience: balanced owners + renters (PRODUCT.md). Job: browse → unit → chat-book; owner: upload → specs → publish. Constraints: AR RTL + EN LTR parity, Meta-only AI, partial media on uploaded units, code-led build.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Offisio is a Cairo poster wall of layered sheets — the real room pasted on top, the emptied room and fit concepts torn through beneath it. It refuses the identical-card listing grid and the neutral SaaS template.

OWN-WORLD: Paste-gray board ground (#D8D3C6), black ink (#211D17), chalk blue (#2F5468), brick (#A63A2A), mustard (#C99A3C); pale torn edge (#F3EDDD) is the only divider — jagged torn strips between layers, flat ink bands behind headlines, black-chip small labels. Display: Cairo 900, tight; body: system sans. Operate screens use flat sheets with one torn strip at the top, never full collage.

STORY: A renter sees a real office sheet, tears to the emptied room, tears again to their fit (startup, clinic, training), then books in chat. An owner pastes a new sheet onto the wall: video in, specs and listing printed onto it, staged layers added under the same torn edge.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Board ground; masthead as pasted strip (brand in black ink, nav as flat labels, Chat to book a brick block); huge two-line display headline in black and chalk blue; featured unit as one large sheet whose photo tears at the bottom into its emptied layer; listings below as offset pasted sheets with ink-band price strips.

FORM: Challenger (won both axes over the assigned direction) — décollage torn billboard, catalog design-canon-decollage-torn-billboard, over grounded #1 broker listing sheet (kept: ruled spec lines, serial district stamps). seed:61aa4d49ce200241. Raises: from neon circuit, the torn edge is one continuous reveal line through the viewer; from darkroom, upload progress reads as ordered station strips; from raku, pipeline stage labels stack like kiln logs on the upload sheet.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
